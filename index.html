Lab note 14: soft-body laces

We don't chase trends or ship the same site twice. We pick one strange, tactile idea and sweat every frame of it: the weight of a click, the wobble of a ring, the half-second before a block lands.

Strategy, art direction, 3D, sound and engineering sit at one table, so the idea never gets lost in a hand-off. Small team, short lines, long tails.
